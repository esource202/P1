About Me

Creative mind.
Practical approach.

I'm Md Rakibul Hasan Rakib — known simply as Rakib — a creative professional based in Siddhirganj, Narayanganj, Bangladesh. My work sits where design, technology and business meet: graphic design, AI-powered creative work, web design and digital product development.

For around three years I've been designing logos, brand visuals and advertising materials. Over the last year and a half, I've brought AI tools into my workflow — AI image generation, prompt engineering and AI-assisted advertising creatives — to work faster and explore new creative possibilities.

Alongside creative work, I'm a computer operator, VAT consultant and businessman, currently in the 3rd year of my degree. This mix keeps my design decisions practical and business-aware. I continuously learn new technologies — AI, web technologies, creative design and digital business — and turn what I learn into useful digital projects and solutions.
