I'm Rakib — a multi-disciplinary creative professional from Siddhirganj, Narayanganj, combining graphic design, AI-powered creativity, web design and real business knowledge to build digital work that actually solves problems.

With around 3 years of experience in graphic design and 1.5 years working with AI tools, I create everything from brand identities and advertising visuals to modern responsive websites and practical digital products. I'm currently completing my degree (3rd year running) while continuously learning new technologies and developing practical digital projects. For me, design isn't decoration — it's a tool to help businesses communicate, sell and grow.
